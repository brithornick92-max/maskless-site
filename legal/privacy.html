Privacy Policy

Effective Date: December 12, 2025
Company: Maskless App LLC (“Maskless”, “we”, “us”, “our”)

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use the Maskless app, website, and related services (“Service”). Maskless is built for anonymous expression; we intentionally minimize what we collect.

Quick summary: No email/phone is required. We do not use human moderators—only automated safety filters. Images are required for posts. A single Explicit/Sexual toggle blurs content and routes sexually explicit posts to the Flirt/Play feed. If you enable local features, others may see only an approximate distance—never your exact location.

1) Information We Collect

A) Account & Profile

  • Anonymous username (auto-generated; you may change it; not guaranteed unique)
  • Optional profile elements (e.g., avatar)

B) Content You Provide

  • Posts, images (required), comments, reactions/likes, shares
  • Direct messages (Chats)
  • Support emails/messages you send to us
  • Settings and preferences (blocks, keyword filters, safety toggles)

C) Calm & Check-In Inputs

  • Calm: breathwork patterns you configure; usage of meditations, calming images/sounds, bubble tapping, and drawing tools
  • Check-In: mood, feelings, and body check selections used to generate your affirmation, suggested steps, and journal prompt (an optional sexual set appears only when applicable and enabled)

D) Device & Log Information

  • Device identifiers (internal token), app/OS version, crash logs, diagnostics
  • IP address and approximate location derived from IP (for security/safety)
  • Usage data (features used, timestamps)

E) Optional Access (with permission)

  • Photo library (to choose an image)
  • Camera/microphone (if you use a feature that needs them)
  • Precise location only if you enable a feature that requires it (e.g., local proximity)

2) How We Use Information

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service (deliver posts/DMs; render and store images)
  • Apply automated safety filters (blur explicit content; flag/limit certain words/phrases; restrict distribution when appropriate)
  • Personalize Calm and Check-In outputs from your inputs (affirmations, suggested steps, journal prompts; sexual set only when applicable and enabled)
  • Prevent fraud/abuse, protect users, and enforce Terms/Guidelines
  • Troubleshooting, analytics, and product development
  • Legal compliance

3) Sharing

  • Service providers: hosting, storage, delivery, analytics, crash reporting—under instructions and appropriate safeguards
  • Legal/safety: when required by law or needed to prevent harm
  • Business transfers: successors/affiliates in a merger/acquisition with protections
  • No sale: we do not sell personal information

4) Anonymity & What Other Users See

Other users see your username and the content you choose to share. They never see device identifiers, IP addresses, or precise location.

5) Explicit/Sexual Content, Blur & Routing

  • Single toggle: one checkbox for “Explicit/Sexual.”
  • Blurred by default: explicit posts are blurred with a tap-to-reveal interstitial.
  • Routing: sexually explicit posts must appear in the Flirt/Play feed; the app enforces this.
  • Prohibited: sexual content involving minors, exploitation, non-consensual material, and illegal content.

6) No Human Moderators; Automated Safety

We do not use human moderators. Automated systems may scan text and images to enforce rules (e.g., blur explicit content; flag/limit certain words/phrases; restrict distribution of unsafe material). You can block users, filter keywords, and report content.

7) Location

By default, we do not collect precise location. If you enable a location-based feature, we ask permission and process only what’s needed. Other users may see an approximate distance—not exact coordinates or direction. You can revoke location access in device settings.

8) Photos, Camera, Microphone, and Files

With permission, we access only what a feature needs (e.g., a photo you pick). Content is uploaded only when you choose to share it. You can revoke permissions any time.

9) Push Notifications & Tokens

If you enable notifications, your device’s push token is used only to deliver messages you opt into (e.g., replies, safety alerts). You can turn notifications off in device or in-app settings.

10) Analytics, Diagnostics & Crash Reports

We use privacy-respecting analytics and diagnostics to understand feature performance and fix issues (e.g., crash reports, error logs, performance metrics). Where required, we obtain consent and you can adjust analytics preferences in Settings. Analytics data is de-identified or aggregated where possible.

11) Cookies (Website Only)

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies for core functionality, analytics, and security. Manage preferences via your browser and any site banner. The mobile app does not use web cookies.

12) Your Choices & Controls

  • Update your username and preferences in the app
  • Delete your posts; request account deletion via support@masklessapp.com
  • Enable/disable the Check-In sexual set; hide sexual feelings
  • Use keyword filters; block or report users
  • Manage location permissions in device settings
  • Manage subscriptions in App Store/Google Play settings
  • Depending on your region (GDPR/CCPA/etc.), request access, correction, deletion, portability, or objection via legal@masklessapp.com

13) Data Retention

  • Posts/media: until you delete them; residual copies may persist in backups for up to ~90 days
  • Account & identifiers: while your account is active and up to 24 months after deletion
  • Logs & security data: typically 12–24 months (longer if needed for investigations/legal obligations)

We may retain de-identified or aggregate data indefinitely.

14) Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards, including encryption in transit and at rest where applicable. No system is 100% secure.

15) Children

The Service is for adults 18+. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. Sexual content involving minors is strictly prohibited and may be reported to authorities.

16) Law Enforcement & Legal Requests

We carefully review legal requests and disclose information only as required by law, court order, or to prevent imminent harm. We may notify affected users unless prohibited or unsafe.

17) International Transfers

Your information may be processed and stored in the United States or other countries that may have different data protection laws than your country. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards (e.g., standard contractual clauses).

18) Changes

We may update this Policy. When we do, we will revise the Effective Date and, if changes are material, provide additional notice (e.g., in-app). Continued use constitutes acceptance.
