Maskless – Frequently Asked Questions

Welcome to Maskless! Below you’ll find answers to the most common questions about how the app works, how your anonymity is protected, and what you can expect from each feature.

✨ GENERAL

What is Maskless?

Maskless is an anonymous social-wellbeing app for adults (18+). It combines community support, connection, playful flirting, and calming tools all in one place. Every post is anonymous. No usernames or profiles are shown publicly.

Who can use Maskless?

Adults age 18+ only. The app is not intended for minors.

Do I need an account?

You do not need a traditional account with a profile or public identity. Maskless creates a private, anonymous ID so your posts, likes, comments, follows, and chats stay connected to you without exposing who you are.

🖼 POSTING & FEEDS

Where do my posts appear?

Maskless has three main posting feeds. When you post, you choose your Vibe:

  • Connect — everyday thoughts, experiences, humor, general sharing
  • Support — vulnerable moments, emotional help, compassionate responses
  • Flirt — playful, lighthearted, or sexual content

Your Vibe determines which feed the post goes into.

📸 DO I NEED AN IMAGE TO POST?

Yes. All posts on Maskless require an image.

You can upload your own photo, choose one from your library, or generate an AI image inside the app. This helps keep the feed visually engaging and makes every post stand out.

🔞 EXPLICIT & SEXUAL CONTENT

Maskless uses one single setting for sensitive content:

“Explicit Content (includes sexual)”

How does it work?

  • If you check the Explicit box → your post will be blurred by default.
  • Users must tap to reveal.
  • This protects everyone from unwanted triggers.

Where can explicit posts go?

Sexual Explicit Content

  • Must be posted in Flirt only.
  • Must have the Explicit box checked.
  • Always blurred until tapped.

If you try to post sexual content into Connect or Support, the app will redirect you to Flirt.

Non-Sexual Explicit Content

This may be posted in any feed (Connect, Support, Flirt) as long as the explicit box is checked.

🙈 ARE POSTS MODERATED?

No human moderators. Maskless does not have live human moderation.

Instead, the app uses:

  • Keyword flagging for dangerous or harmful content
  • Automatic hiding for unsafe phrases
  • Automatic blurring of all explicit content
  • User tools to block or report others

💬 COMMENTS, LIKES & SHARING

Can users comment on posts?

Yes. You can like posts, comment on them, and engage freely — all anonymously.

Can I share someone’s post?

Yes, users can share posts inside the app.

📨 CHATS (DMs)

What are Chats?

Chats are Maskless's private direct messages. They are separate from the posting feeds.

You can:

  • Chat one-on-one
  • Send images
  • Enjoy anonymous, private conversation

🧘 CALM TOOLS

What’s inside the Calm section?

Calm is your soothing space inside Maskless. It includes:

  • Synced Breathing Circle — inhale / hold / exhale animations
  • Custom breathwork patterns
  • Gentle random cues
  • Calming images with meditation sounds
  • Bubble-tapping relaxation
  • Drawing tools — pens, markers, watercolor, charcoal, pastels

📝 CHECK-IN

What happens when I check in?

You choose your:

  • Mood
  • Feelings
  • Body sensations via a tappable body map

You then receive:

  • Affirmations
  • Suggested next steps
  • Journal prompt

What about sexual feelings?

If you include a positive sexual feeling with no negative feelings, you’ll receive:

  • Sexual affirmations
  • Sexual suggested next steps
  • Sexual journal prompts

You can turn sexual content off by disabling the sexual feeling toggle at the top of Check-In.

👤 FOLLOWING, PRIVACY & CONTROL

Can I follow other users?

Yes, users can follow one another anonymously.

Can I approve followers?

Yes, you can require manual approval if you prefer.

Can I share my profile?

Yes — you have a shareable profile link others can follow.

What if someone makes me uncomfortable?

You can:

  • Block them
  • Report their content
  • Use keyword filters to hide topics

🔐 SAFETY & ANONYMITY

Is Maskless actually anonymous?

Yes. No names, no public profiles, and no identifying information.

Can anyone see my identity?

No — not even in chats.

What happens if a post is dangerous?

Specific words and phrases are automatically hidden or flagged to protect users.

📱 TECHNICAL

Is Maskless available on iOS and Android?

Yes — both App Store and Google Play.

Does Maskless collect my data?

No personal identity is ever collected. Only your anonymous interactions are stored.
