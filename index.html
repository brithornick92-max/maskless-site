Maskless – Frequently Asked Questions

Welcome to Maskless! Below you’ll find answers to the most common questions about how the app works, how your anonymity is protected, and what you can expect from each feature.

What is Maskless? Who can use it?

Maskless is an anonymous social-wellbeing app for adults (18+). It combines community support, connection, playful flirting, and calming tools all in one place. Every post is anonymous. No usernames or profiles are shown publicly.

Do I need an account or login?

You don’t need a traditional public profile. Maskless creates a private, anonymous ID so your posts, likes, comments, follows, and chats stay connected to you without exposing who you are.

Where do my posts appear?

When you post, you choose your Vibe:

  • Connect — everyday thoughts, experiences, humor, general sharing
  • Support — vulnerable moments, emotional help, compassionate responses
  • Flirt — playful, lighthearted, or sexual content
Do I need an image to post?

Yes. All posts on Maskless require an image. Upload your own, choose one from your library, or generate an AI image inside the app.

How does Explicit/Sexual content work?

There’s a single toggle: Explicit Content (includes sexual).

  • If checked → your post is blurred by default (tap to reveal).
  • Sexually explicit posts must go in Flirt; non-sexual explicit can be in any feed if blurred.
Are posts moderated by humans?

No human moderators. Automated systems blur explicit content, flag unsafe phrases, and limit distribution when needed. Users can block, filter keywords, and report content.

What about comments, likes, and sharing?

You can like and comment anonymously. Posts can be shared within the app.

What are Chats?

Private DMs for one-on-one conversation (with optional images), separate from the feeds.

What’s inside Calm?
  • Synced Breathing Circle (customizable patterns)
  • Short meditations with calming images/sounds
  • Bubble-tapping relaxation
  • Drawing tools (pens, watercolor, charcoal, pastels)
How does Check-In work?

You pick your mood, feelings, and body sensations; you’ll get an affirmation, suggested next steps, and a journal prompt. If you choose a positive sexual feeling (and enable sexual content), you’ll get sexual versions of those.

Can I follow people? What if someone makes me uncomfortable?

Yes, you can follow others anonymously (and require approval if you prefer). You can block users, report content, and set keyword filters.

Is Maskless truly anonymous?

Yes. No public names or profiles. Other users never see your device info, IP, or precise location.

Is it on iOS and Android? Do you collect my data?

Yes—both stores. We minimize data and do not collect personal identity. Anonymous interactions are stored to run the app.
